# Procedure: Judge as a Shark

Use this procedure to judge one pitch as one Shark model.

## 1. Read the Shark file

1. Read `sharks/<name>.md` from start to end.
2. Note the lens, the beliefs, the dealbreakers, and the "Where this lens misfires" section.
3. Note the offer rate in the deal record. Most pitches get OUT.

## 2. Find past pitches

1. Open `precedents/<name>.md`.
2. Go to the section for the industry on the pitch card.
3. Find the 6 past pitches that are most similar to this pitch. Compare the product, the ask, and the valuation.
4. Include at least one past pitch that got an offer, if the section has one.
5. Note the ID and the decision of each past pitch.

## 3. Judge

1. Apply each belief of the Shark to the pitch card.
2. Apply each dealbreaker. A dealbreaker that applies gives OUT.
3. Compare the pitch to the 6 past pitches. Do not copy their decisions.
4. If a "misfires" item applies to this pitch, adjust your judgment and say so.
5. Decide IN or OUT.
6. Give the probability of an offer, from 0 to 1. Start from the offer rate in the deal record. Move up or down only for a clear reason.
7. If IN, give the offer terms. Use the typical check and equity in the deal record as a guide.
8. If OUT, give no offer terms. Give one useful piece of advice.

## 4. Write the verdict

1. Copy `templates/verdict.md`.
2. Fill in each field.
3. Write the objections and the reasoning from the lens of this Shark only. Do not give general advice.
4. In "Evidence", write the belief IDs and the past pitch IDs that caused the decision.
5. Quote only lines from the Shark file. Copy each line exactly, with its source ID.

## 5. Rules for this procedure

- Do not use information about other Sharks.
- Do not use web search.
- Do not try to find the real company or the real episode.
